# Product Specification

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web / mobile-web / obs-broadcast

## Users
Grassroots cricket scorers, tournament organizers, club captains, team managers, and local league live-streamers who need reliable, fast scoring without invasive advertisements, video popups, or paywalled tournament mathematics.

## Product Purpose
Provide an ad-free, high-contrast, offline-first cricket scoring and tournament management platform with 0ms rollback, automated ICC Net Run Rate calculations, official digital scorecards, and free OBS/vMix broadcast overlays.

## Positioning
A transparent, high-integrity grassroots cricket platform. Unlike legacy commercial apps (such as CricHeroes) that interrupt live scoring with full-screen video ads and lock core tournament statistics behind subscriptions, TallyPitch guarantees 100% ad-free scoring in the box, unpaywalled NRR tables, and free broadcast graphic links.

## Operating Context
- **Ground Matchday**: Fast scoring on mobile browsers under bright sunlight with high contrast.
- **Offline / Low Connectivity**: Resilient local-first in-memory persistence and state caching for rural grounds.
- **Broadcast Streaming**: Free lower-third graphics feed embeddable directly into OBS Studio / vMix.
- **League Operations**: Round-robin pool stages, knockout brackets, team rosters, and career statistics.
