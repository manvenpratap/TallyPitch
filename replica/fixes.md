# Fixes and Positioning Plan: CricHeroes Clone

Generated by `/replica-entrepreneur` from real customer reviews across App Store, Google Play, and community discussions.

---

## 1. The Three Lists

### What They Hate (Complaints)
1. **Intrusive Interstitial Video Ads Between Overs**
   - *Problem*: Full-screen video ads pop up automatically between overs or when navigating between tournaments, causing scorers to miss deliveries on the ground.
   - *Evidence*: 2 reviews across 2 sources (App Store, Google Play), avg rating 2.0*.
   - *Quote*: *"Full screen video ads between overs cause scorers to miss the first ball of the next over while trying to close the ad."* ([Google Play Review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cricheroes))
2. **Aggressive Feature Paywalls & Nickel-and-Diming**
   - *Problem*: Basic statistical charts (wagon wheel, opponent scouting, team comparisons) and stream tools are locked behind expensive tiers.
   - *Evidence*: 4 reviews across 3 sources (App Store, Google Play, Reddit), avg rating 1.3*.
   - *Quote*: *"Grassroots tournament organizers need a clean scoring app that doesn't nickel and dime you for live streaming overlays or hide basic player statistics behind expensive paywalls."* ([Reddit Discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Cricket/comments/cricheroes_alternatives))
3. **Data Loss During Sync Glitches**
   - *Problem*: When ground internet drops, entire innings get wiped or corrupted due to remote-dependent sync architectures.
   - *Evidence*: 2 reviews across 2 sources, avg rating 1.0*.
   - *Quote*: *"Scorer lost 18 overs of ball-by-ball entries with no offline backup or local recovery."* ([Google Play Review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cricheroes))

---

### What Is Missing (Direct Requests)
1. **Reliable Offline-First Scoring & Instant Undo**
   - *Request*: Local offline persistence that never depends on a live cellular connection to record deliveries, plus instant rollback.
   - *Quote*: *"Needs an offline scoring mode with instant local undo."* ([App Store Review](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/cricheroes-cricket-scorer/id1084938198))
2. **Built-in OBS / vMix Streaming Overlays Included Free**
   - *Request*: Broadcast graphic templates included without separate per-match fees.
   - *Quote*: *"Paid for the live stream package but the overlay crashed mid match and then they charged extra just to download video highlights. Why separate charges for basic stream tools?"* ([App Store Review](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/cricheroes-cricket-scorer/id1084938198))

---

### What Is Unsolved (Positioning Opportunities)
1. **Club & Grassroots Independence**: Organizers want their own branded league without CricHeroes's intrusive merchandise store ("The Dressing Room") and commercial third-party ads.
2. **Speed & Battery Efficiency**: Scorers on hot afternoon grounds need a fast, low-battery PWA rather than a heavy 150MB native app with heavy background tracking.
