# TallyPitch - Launch & Go-To-Market Plan

Generated by `/replica-launch` to execute an authentic, community-driven launch focused on grassroots cricket organizers and frustrated competitor users.

---

## 1. Pre-Launch Beta & Quality Gate

- **Beta Cohort Size**: 15 grassroots tournament organizers & active scorers.
  - 5 Club scorers in Mumbai / Pune club cricket circuits.
  - 5 Weekend league organizers in Bengaluru / Chennai corporate leagues.
  - 5 Grassroots village and Saturday league scorers in the UK & Australia.
- **Observability & Error Tracking**:
  - Client-side error telemetry (Sentry / LogRocket) tracking ball entry exceptions and undo inconsistencies.
  - Server-side health monitoring on `/api/health` and WebSocket connection drop rates.
- **Success Criteria Before Public Launch**:
  - Zero crashes during 20-over continuous scoring sessions.
  - Zero data loss across simulated offline network disconnect tests.
  - 100% test pass rate on automated Playwright engine test suite.

## 3. Product Hunt & Hacker News Launch Strategy

### Show HN Post Draft
- **Title**: `Show HN: TallyPitch – An ad-free, offline-first cricket scoring and tournament platform`
- **Body**:
  > Hi HN,  
  >
  > If you’ve ever scored a local cricket match on a sunny field, you know the frustration: the bowler is at the top of their mark, you tap your phone, and a 30-second unskippable video ad takes over your screen. You miss the ball, the teams argue over the total, and you're stuck guessing.  
  >
  > We built **TallyPitch** to solve this. It is a fast, offline-first digital cricket scoring platform and tournament manager designed specifically for grassroots clubs.  
  >
  > What makes it different:  
  > 1. **Zero Full-Screen Video Ads**: The scoring console is an ad-free workspace.  
  > 2. **Instant 0ms Rollback**: An in-memory atomic stack restores exact striker/non-striker positions, scores, and bowler figures with one tap.  
  > 3. **Offline-First Resilience**: All deliveries persist in local memory first, so spotty cellular coverage on rural grounds never wipes an innings.  
  > 4. **Free Broadcast Overlays**: A clean OBS/vMix browser source URL is included for community livestreams with zero per-match surcharges.  
  > 5. **Automated Net Run Rate (NRR)**: Multi-stage pool tables calculate official ICC tie-breakers in real time.  
  >
  > Web app is live: https://tallypitch.app  
  >
  > We’d love feedback from anyone who plays or scores amateur sports.

### Product Hunt Launch
- **Tagline**: The transparent grassroots cricket scoring and league platform. Zero ads in the box.
- **Category**: Sports Tech, Productivity, Web Apps.
- **Hero Image / Video**: 45-second walkthrough showing toss, ball entry, strike swap, undo rollback, and live OBS stream preview.
