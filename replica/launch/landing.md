## 2. The Problem: What Grassroots Scorers Dread

*Paraphrased from real organizer and scorer feedback across cricket communities:*

### Problem 1: Missed Deliveries from Full-Screen Video Popups
When a bowler starts their run-up for the next over, the last thing a scorer needs is an unskippable 30-second video advertisement taking over the entire screen. Scorers on hot afternoon grounds struggle to close the popup, miss the first ball of the over, and are forced into awkward guesswork.

### Problem 2: Paywalls on Basic Tournament Integrity
Grassroots clubs shouldn't be nickel-and-dimed for core tournament math. Locking team head-to-head records, MVP rankings, or Net Run Rate tables behind aggressive subscription tiers fractures local cricket communities. Paying extra per match just to stream a lower-third graphics overlay penalizes volunteer organizers broadcasting to club supporters.

## 3. How It Works: Matchday in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Quick Setup & The Toss (30 Seconds)
Pick your teams, choose custom overs (5 to 20 overs), select the ball type (tennis, leather, tape), and record the toss with our interactive 3D coin flip. Confirm your opening batters and opening bowler.

### Step 2: Live Scoring with Instant 0ms Rollback
Enter deliveries on a high-contrast 6-button keypad. Strike rotates automatically on odd runs (1, 3) and flips at the end of the over. Miskeyed a ball under pressure? Tap Undo to instantly roll back match score, overs, and striker figures with zero latency.

### Step 3: Publish Scorecards & Broadcast Live
Generate an official ICC-standard digital scorecard with fall-of-wickets timeline and live ball-by-ball commentary. Copy your free OBS/vMix overlay URL directly into your streaming software for instant broadcast graphics.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Does TallyPitch work when mobile data drops at the ground?
**A:** Yes. TallyPitch is engineered offline-first. Every ball, run, extra, and wicket is stored locally in device memory first. You can score an entire 20-over match without a single byte of internet; when your connection returns, data syncs cleanly.

### Q: Are there really zero pop-up video ads while scoring?
**A:** Yes. We guarantee zero full-screen interstitial video ads in the scoring console across both Free and Pro tiers. Your attention belongs on the bowler and batter, not on closing marketing ads.

### Q: How do I broadcast live scores to YouTube or Facebook?
**A:** In your match settings, copy your unique live stream overlay URL. In OBS Studio, vMix, or Prism Live, add a new **Browser Source**, paste the URL, and position the transparent lower-third scoreboard wherever you like. It updates in real time with zero per-match fees.

### Q: How does the instant rollback undo work?
**A:** TallyPitch maintains an in-memory stack snapshot of the entire match state after every ball. Tapping the Undo button restores the exact scores, overs, striker/non-striker positions, and bowler figures from the previous delivery in 0 milliseconds.

### Q: Can I manage multi-group tournaments with knockouts?
**A:** Yes. The Tournament Hub supports dual-pool structures (Group A & Group B round-robin stages) that automatically calculate Net Run Rate (NRR) and advance top teams into semi-finals and finals.

### Q: Can I cancel a Pro subscription without calling customer service?
**A:** Absolutely. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time with a single click in your Account Settings via the self-serve Stripe Customer Portal. No cancellation fees, no surveys, and no phone calls.
