## 2. Reviewer Feedback on Price & Billing

Derived from 10 verified public reviews across App Store, Google Play, and Reddit (see `replica/feedback.md`):

- **4 reviews (40% of all critical reviews, avg rating 1.3*)** specifically condemned aggressive nickel-and-diming:
  - *"Paid for the live stream package but the overlay crashed mid match and then they charged extra just to download video highlights. Why separate charges for basic stream tools?"* (App Store)
  - *"Grassroots tournament organizers need a clean scoring app that doesn't nickel and dime you for live streaming overlays or hide basic player statistics behind expensive paywalls."* (Reddit)
  - *"Upgraded to Pro to test features, but cancelling was deliberately hidden behind multiple unclickable screens and no self-serve portal."* (Google Play)
- **Top User Complaints to Solve**:
  1. Per-match streaming fees penalize weekend clubs broadcasting to parents and members.
  2. Locking Net Run Rate tables and player stats behind paywalls breaks competitive integrity.
  3. Dark patterns around subscription cancellations.

## 4. Product Billing Principles & Anti-Pattern Guarantees

1. **One-Click Self-Serve Cancellation**:  
   Direct link to the Stripe Customer Portal inside Account Settings. Users can cancel or pause in one click with zero dark patterns, no customer service calls, and no survey gates.
2. **Clear Renewal Notice**:  
   Automated reminder email sent 7 days prior to any annual renewal.
3. **No Per-Match Surcharges**:  
   Broadcast lower-third OBS/vMix overlays are included without charging per delivery, per match, or per tournament.
4. **No Ad Interstitials During Play**:  
   The scoring console is an ad-free workspace across both Free and Paid tiers.
