## 1. The Name

### The Positioning Angle (from `replica/fixes.md`)
> **Option B: The Transparent League Platform**  
> *"For cricket club and tournament organizers who hate being nickel-and-dimed for basic stats, team comparisons, and live streaming overlays, **TallyPitch** provides professional ICC scorecards, automated Net Run Rate tables, and broadcast OBS overlays completely free, ad-free, and unpaywalled."*

| Style | Candidates |

### The 5 Finalists (Filtered for TM Clearance & Angle Fit)

1. **TallyPitch** (*Selected Winner*): Compound name fusing "tally" (transparent, immutable ledger, honest counts) and "pitch" (the 22-yard turf). Distinctive, short (3 syllables), punchy, 0 confusing similarity to CricHeroes.
2. **PitchLedger**: Formal, high-integrity, implies audited scoring and unpaywalled league management.
3. **GrassrootsXI**: Pure descriptive brand for community leagues, weekend tournaments, and local clubs.
4. **ScorePavilion**: Digital clubhouse metaphor conveying club tradition and community.
5. **StumpCraft**: Tactile, artisanal, celebrating grassroots cricket fundamentals.

## 4. Logo & Visual Mark Brief

- **Core Concept in One Line**:  
  *A geometric tally counter fused with three dynamic cricket stumps, rendered in royal pitch sapphire and electric cobalt, signifying precision, transparent math, and grassroots authenticity.*
- **Mark Type**: Symbol + Wordmark.
  - *Symbol*: Three vertical rectangular stump pillars standing on a crease line, intersected by a diagonal 45° tally stroke forming a dynamic stylized 'T' and 'P'.
  - *Wordmark*: Clean, modern geometric grotesque (Plus Jakarta Sans 800) with tight tracking and a high x-height.
- **Scale Constraints**:
  - Must remain instantly legible at **16px** (browser favicon) as a 3-stump glyph with zero fine hairlines.
  - Scales up to **1024x1024** for the iOS App Store icon (solid sapphire background, zero transparency, centered symbol).
- **Deliverables Required**:
  1. `logo.svg` (Full horizontal symbol + wordmark)
  2. `icon.svg` (Standalone geometric tally-stumps mark)
  3. `icon-1024.png` (1024x1024 app icon, no alpha channel)
  4. Favicon package (`favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`)
  5. Social OG Image `og-image.png` (1200x630px preview card)
- **Zero Resemblance Guarantee**:
  - CricHeroes uses a green circular shield containing an orange cricket ball and a stylized batsman silhouette.
  - TallyPitch strictly avoids shields, bats, figures, and turf green. TallyPitch is strictly geometric, sapphire blue, and typographical.

## 5. Voice & Copy Guide

### The Three Voice Pillars
1. **Direct, not blunt**: Reports scores, rules, and calculations immediately and factually, without patronizing or robotic prose.
2. **Transparent, not cynical**: Explains all stats, calculations (NRR, MVP, strike rates) openly without manufacturing urgency or whining about competitors.
3. **Dedicated, not ceremonial**: Designed for scorers, umpires, and tournament organizers working under hot afternoon sun; respects user time, battery life, and cognitive load.
