Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does TallyPitch work when mobile data drops at the ground?

Yes. TallyPitch is engineered offline-first. Every ball, run, extra, and wicket is stored locally in device memory first. You can score an entire 20-over match without a single byte of internet; when connection returns, data syncs cleanly.

Are there really zero pop-up video ads while scoring?

Yes. We guarantee zero full-screen interstitial video ads in the scoring console across both Free and Pro tiers. Your attention belongs on the bowler and batter, not on closing marketing ads.

How do I broadcast live scores to YouTube or Facebook?

In your match settings, copy your unique live stream overlay URL. In OBS Studio, vMix, or Prism Live, add a new Browser Source, paste the URL, and position the transparent lower-third scoreboard wherever you like. It updates in real time with zero per-match fees.

How does the instant rollback undo work?

TallyPitch maintains an in-memory stack snapshot of the entire match state after every ball. Tapping the Undo button restores the exact scores, overs, striker/non-striker positions, and bowler figures from the previous delivery in 0 milliseconds.

Can I manage multi-group tournaments with knockouts?

Yes. The Tournament Hub supports dual-pool structures (Group A & Group B round-robin stages) that automatically calculate Net Run Rate (NRR) and advance top teams into semi-finals and finals.

Can I cancel a Pro subscription without calling customer service?

Absolutely. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time with a single click in your Account Settings via the self-serve Stripe Customer Portal. No cancellation fees, no surveys, and no phone calls.
